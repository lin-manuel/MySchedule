Early Decision · Dream school · Binding first choice
Dartmouth College · CS modified with Economics

Dartmouth is the lead story: small-college teaching, flexible D-Plan rhythm, and a modified CS+Economics degree that lets you build technical systems while understanding the markets they enter. That is exactly the Akili Code question: how to build useful educational technology for African contexts with both engineering rigor and economic judgment.

Why Dartmouth CS + EconomicsA technically serious degree that never loses sight of real markets
core

CS side

Algorithms, data structures, systems, HCI, and applied ML: the engineering foundation for turning Akili Code from content into a durable education product.

Economics side

Microeconomics, econometrics, markets, and development-facing economic reasoning: the framework for pricing, adoption, incentives, and access.

Application sentence

Dartmouth is where Immanuel can turn a self-taught builder profile into a deliberate CS+Economics education: rigorous enough for systems work, flexible enough for Kenya leave-term projects, and intimate enough for faculty mentorship.

Secondary application · ORFE
Princeton University · ORFE

Princeton remains a strong second-choice story: applied mathematics, probability, optimization, and financial modeling connect naturally to the routing engine project and Akili Code's systems-builder direction. It supports the Dartmouth-first plan; it does not replace it.

Why ORFE fitsApplied math for real systems

Distinct from Dartmouth

Princeton emphasizes ORFE independent work, operations research, and quantitative finance; Dartmouth emphasizes modified CS+Econ, faculty access, and D-Plan flexibility.
